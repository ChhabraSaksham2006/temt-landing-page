# SEO Keyword Strategy for Carbon Accounting & Emissions Platform

This document outlines the high-intent, feature-specific, and long-tail SEO keywords targeted by top-ranking competitors in the enterprise carbon and emissions accounting software space.

> [!TIP]
> **Focus on Compliance & Scope 3**
> Enterprise buyers are highly motivated by regulatory compliance (CSRD, SEC) and the complex challenge of tracking supply chain emissions (Scope 3). Targeting these pain points yields higher conversion rates.

## 1. Core High-Intent Keywords
These are your primary target keywords for homepage and main product pages.
- Enterprise carbon accounting software
- Carbon management platform for enterprise
- Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions software
- Audit-ready carbon reporting software
- Corporate decarbonization platform
- Transport emissions management tool

## 2. Regulatory & Compliance Keywords
Incorporate these into your content strategy as enterprises search for tools to meet legal mandates.
- CSRD reporting software
- SEC climate disclosure software
- GHG Protocol compliant software
- ISSB reporting tools
- Automated regulatory compliance software

## 3. Technical & "Problem-Solving" Keywords
Engineering and finance leaders search for terms that address data integration and quality.
- Automated carbon data collection software
- Scope 3 supply chain emissions tracking
- ERP-integrated carbon accounting
- Carbon emissions data pipeline
- AI-powered emissions factor management
- Freight emissions calculator
- Fleet emissions tracking

## 4. Problem-Solving Content Topics (For Blog/Resources)
Enterprise buyers often search for the *problems* they are trying to solve. These make excellent blog posts or whitepapers:
- How to automate Scope 3 transport emissions data
- Best practices for audit-ready carbon reporting
- Streamlining GHG protocol compliance
- Logistics carbon footprint optimization

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Audit-Readiness is Key**
> Since the platform is targeted at finance teams who need to "answer for the numbers", use phrases like "audit-ready," "traceable methodologies," and "compliance-grade data" extensively. This addresses the #1 concern of enterprise sustainability and finance teams.
